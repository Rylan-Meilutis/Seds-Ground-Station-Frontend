# 0.4.0 build 40

Version 0.4.0 marks the features added since 0.3.1 (proximity lock,
recovery search, replays without a station, flight plans, crew voice and the
new station modes). The build number keeps counting from 39.

Needs a Ground Station on the current `dev` stack. Boards and station are
updated together; mixing this build with an older station is not supported.

**Requires iOS 15 or later and Android 7 or later.**

## New since build 39

- **Android 15 and 16: the top bar works again.** On these Android versions
  the app's top bar, ABORT included, sat under the phone's status bar and
  only its bottom edge responded to a tap. The app now stays clear of the
  status bar, navigation bar and camera cutout, and moves up when the keyboard
  opens.
- **Proximity lock and recovery search settings save again.** Turning the
  proximity lock on or off, and the recovery search area, swath and
  "Clear all tracks", were refused for every administrator ("Original ordinary
  policy owner is missing").
- **Android: the proximity lock and recovery search notifications show.** On
  Android 13 and later the app now asks for permission to show notifications
  when either starts, and their ongoing notifications appear as soon as you
  allow it.
- **The dashboard no longer stays on "Loading layout...".** Right after
  signing in, the dashboard could wait forever for its layout; it now asks
  the station again.
- **Launch refusals explained.** If the station refuses a Launch before
  anything was sent to the rocket, it now says so ("Launch refused, nothing was
  sent; Launch is available again") and Launch can be pressed again. The
  safety reference in the app lists this case.
- **More reliable builds.** The Android, Wear OS and Windows builds no longer
  fail when a download server is busy, so releases reach the stores more
  reliably. No change to how the app works.
