# 0.4.0 build 41

Needs a Ground Station on the current `dev` stack. Boards and station are
updated together; mixing this build with an older station is not supported.

**Requires iOS 15 or later and Android 7 or later.**

## New since build 40

- **Apple Watch app on TestFlight.** The iPhone build now includes the GS
  watch app and its push-to-talk widget. Install the iPhone app from
  TestFlight and the watch app installs on your paired Apple Watch (or from
  the Watch app's Available Apps list).
- **More reliable builds.** A release check no longer fails on one of our
  build servers because of a setting only present there. No change to how the
  app works.
